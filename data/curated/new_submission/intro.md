The gender pay gap is the difference between the average pay of men and women in an organisation. In the UK, employers with more than 250 workers must report their organisation's gender pay gap. In this extract of the data submitted, data is provided for companies registered in Scotland between the 2017/2018 and 2025/2026 financial years.

> Employers can publish a voluntary action plan alongside their gender pay gap data. The purpose of action plans is to help employers improve gender equality at work.
